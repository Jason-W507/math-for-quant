# Capstone limitations

- The input is synthetic and cannot support a claim about any real asset or venue.
- Three test rows demonstrate audit mechanics but cannot establish generalization.
- Gross returns are additive contributions on one declared capital base, not compounded holding-period returns.
- Commission, spread, and impact are fixed teaching values rather than calibrated cost models.
- The `2024-07-v1` trading calendar is a versioned fixture, not an official historical calendar service.
- Passing the package proves reproducibility and gate sensitivity, not profitability, production readiness, or future validity.
