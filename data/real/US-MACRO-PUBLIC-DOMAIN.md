# U.S. macrodata snapshot

The fixed snapshot `stat-arb-us-macro-1999q4-2009q3.json` is derived from
the `statsmodels.datasets.macrodata` dataset. The statsmodels dataset page
identifies the underlying sources as FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)
and the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and marks the dataset **public domain**.

Source: https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/datasets/generated/macrodata.html

The snapshot is included only to exercise time ordering, provenance, hashing,
and an external-data estimation path. It is not a tradable pair or an alpha claim.
